PRIVACY POLICY — Capy Island
Last updated: 15 October 2026 (parties and contests added)

Capy Island is a free game made by Lydia Dover ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game keeps, why, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, leave a comment on this page.

WHAT THE GAME KEEPS

- Your island: your progress, items, hobbies and settings, and the name you give in the game. It is saved on your device. When you're online it is also saved in Unity Cloud Save, so it isn't lost and can be opened on another device.
- A player ID: the first time you play, Unity Authentication gives the game a random player ID, so your island can be kept for you. It doesn't contain your name or email, and you don't have to sign in to play.
- If you choose to make an account:
  - Username and password: they are handled by Unity Authentication. I never see your password.
  - Continue with Google: Google confirms who you are and Unity Authentication links your Google account's ID to your island, so you can sign in on another device. The game doesn't keep your name, email or anything else from your Google account.
- Your friends list: the friends you add with a friend code, and friend requests you send or receive.

WHAT OTHER PLAYERS CAN SEE

Playing with others is only with people you choose: someone who has your friend code, or the code of a private server you share.

- Your friend code: the name you give in the game and a few numbers (like Miso#4821). Anyone you give it to can send you a friend request. You decide whether to accept.
- Your friends can see: your in-game name, your capybara's outfit, how far your island has grown, whether you're playing right now, and a copy of your island so they can visit it.
- On a live visit, everyone on the island with you sees your capybara walking about, the emojis you send, and what you put down on the island you're on. There is no typed chat.
- On a private server, everyone who entered the same server code sees your in-game name, your outfit and that you're online, and can send you a friend request.
- Parties and contests: when you host one, everyone on your server is invited to your island: your friends, and everyone on your private server (friends or not). Whoever comes sees a copy of your island, the other guests, and (in a contest) everyone's name and score. When you're invited, you choose whether to go. Guests also see each other's friend code, so they can send each other a friend request.
- Nothing about you is shown to anyone else: only to your friends and to people on a private server with you.

WHAT THE GAME DOESN'T DO

- No ads, no tracking, and no analytics are built into the game.
- No real-money purchases.
- Your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

WHY

Only to save your island, let you open it again on this device or another one, and let you play with the friends you choose. Nothing else.

SERVICES THE GAME USES

These companies handle some information for the game, under their own privacy policies:
- Unity (Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Friends, Lobby and Relay, which connect friends' games; Unity, the engine the game is made with, may also collect basic technical data such as device type and crash reports): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Google (only if you choose Continue with Google): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- itch.io (if you play or download the game there): https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy

Their servers may be in other countries than yours, including the United States.

HOW LONG IT'S KEPT, AND DELETING IT

Your island stays in the cloud until you delete it. Apart from your player ID, account and friends list, everything the game keeps about you is your island, which you can see in the game (and copy with Settings > Export).

- To remove a friend, tap the X next to them in Neighbours. To leave a private server, choose "Leave server".
- To delete your island from the cloud and your account (with its username or Google sign-in, the copy your friends can visit, and all your friendships), open Settings in the game and choose "Delete my account". It happens straight away and can't be undone.
- To delete the island on your device too, choose "Start over" in Settings, or uninstall the game (in a browser, clear the site's data).
- If you can't open the game any more, leave a comment on this page and I'll help you delete it.

CHILDREN

Capy Island can be played without giving any personal information. Children under 13 (or the age your country sets) should only make an account, or share friend codes and server codes, with a parent's or guardian's permission. There is no typed chat, and only people a child shares a code with can play with them. Someone who hosts a party or contest on a private server invites everyone on it, so a private server's code should only be shared with people the child knows. A parent can delete a child's account and island at any time with "Delete my account" in Settings.

SECURITY

Information sent between the game and Unity is encrypted. No system is perfectly safe, but only what's needed to keep your island is kept.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
